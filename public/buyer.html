not connected

Two different balances. Holding USDC in your wallet is not enough to pay an x402 invoice. Gateway payments are debited from a balance you have deposited into the GatewayWallet contract ahead of time — that is what lets the payment settle off-chain with no gas and no tx from you. A wallet with $5 of USDC and an empty Gateway deposit still gets 402 {"reason":"insufficient_balance"} on a $0.01 call.

Wallet (EOA) balance
—
On-chain USDC held by your address. Pays gas and funds deposits. Not spendable by x402.
Gateway (deposited) balance
—
Deposited into GatewayWallet. This is what /hello-world actually draws from.
USDC

One-time setup (until you spend it down): approve the GatewayWallet to move your USDC, then deposit(token, value). Two wallet confirmations, both on-chain, both paid in gas by you. On Arc Testnet the deposit becomes spendable in about half a second; other chains take minutes. Need testnet USDC first? Circle faucet.
